Characters in Gang Beasts

In Gang Beasts, players control colorful and customizable characters called Beasts. The characters have a soft, flexible appearance that makes their movements funny and unpredictable during fights.

Example of character movement in Gang Beasts.

Character Customization

Players can customize their Beast with different outfits, costumes, colors, hats, and other accessories. This allows players to create their own unique-looking character.

Example of character customization in Gang Beasts.

How the Characters Move

The characters use ragdoll-style physics, meaning they can stumble, fall, grab onto objects, and get knocked around during matches. Their unusual movement is one of the main things that makes Gang Beasts chaotic and funny.

Why the Characters Are Important

The characters are a major part of Gang Beasts' identity. Their simple designs and unpredictable movements make every fight feel different, especially when multiple players are fighting at once.
